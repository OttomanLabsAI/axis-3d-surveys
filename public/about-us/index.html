About us

Our mission is simple: to give Architects, Engineers, and Contractors the data they need to design and build with confidence.

88+ projects delivered

Axis 3D Surveys delivers precise 3D laser scanning, BIM modelling, and as-built verification services across the UK. We help architects, engineers, and contractors design with confidence and accuracy.

From small refurbishments to large-scale infrastructure, we provide clear, reliable information that reduces risk, saves time, and supports smarter decisions. With over 88 projects delivered, we’ve built a reputation for turning complex environments into simple, accurate, and actionable digital models.

We combine advanced scanner instruments and software with trusted expertise to deliver precise, reliable, and future-ready survey solutions.
