Project Summary

Full property survey and walkthrough for developer client. Deliverables included LOD200 Revit model, plans, sections, elevations and site topography.

Challenges

  • Limited access to property due to tenants, assumed flat layouts.
  • Capturing complex roof structure where a loft conversion was going to be undertaken, rigorous accuracy required when modelling in 3D.
